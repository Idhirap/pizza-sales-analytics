# Pizza Sales Analytics – Requirements

## Objective

Analyze pizza sales data to understand sales performance, customer ordering patterns, and the performance of different pizza categories, sizes, and individual pizzas.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

1. Total Revenue
2. Average Order Value
3. Total Pizzas Sold
4. Total Orders
5. Average Pizzas Sold per Order

## Charts & Analysis

### 1. Daily Trend for Total Orders

Analyze the number of orders received for each day of the week.

### 2. Hourly Trend for Total Orders

Analyze the number of orders received during different hours of the day.

### 3. Percentage of Sales by Pizza Category

Determine the percentage contribution of each pizza category to total sales.

### 4. Percentage of Sales by Pizza Size

Determine the percentage contribution of each pizza size to total sales.

### 5. Total Pizzas Sold by Pizza Category

Compare the number of pizzas sold across different pizza categories.

### 6. Top 5 Best Sellers

Identify the top 5 pizzas based on total pizzas sold.

### 7. Bottom 5 Worst Sellers

Identify the bottom 5 pizzas based on total pizzas sold.
